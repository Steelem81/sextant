Data Restrictions - 
    - No PHI
    - No product of Signos intellectual algorithms

Requirements - 
    - Transforms to house precleaned data to ensure follow data restrictions

    - SQL Generator - Claude
    - Logging system to audit:
        - Who asked what question
        - What was the SQL generated
        - What was the response provided
    - Visualization engine
